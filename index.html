— ABOUT

Modeling the process, then controlling it

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Chemical Engineering at Lamar University, working as a Graduate Research Assistant on advanced process control (APC) and optimization for natural gas, LNG, and NGL fractionation systems.

My work spans building steady-state and dynamic simulation models in Aspen Plus and Aspen Dynamics, designing multivariable control strategies with DMC3 Builder, and modeling fractionation columns, de-methanizers, de-ethanizers, de-propanizers, and butane splitters, to evaluate real process performance.

Before this, I supported plant operations and troubleshooting at a chlor-alkali facility, and I've completed formal training in process safety and consequence/risk analysis. I presented my NGL fractionation research at the 2026 AIChE Spring Meeting in Houston.

— PROJECTS

Graduate course & term projects

Simulation, optimization, safety, transport, and data-driven modeling projects completed during my Ph.D. coursework at Lamar University.

♨

PROCESS SIMULATION · ASPEN PLUS V12.5

High-Temperature Heat Pump — Working-Fluid Evaluation

Screened seven candidate refrigerants — including R245fa, R601, and low-GWP HFO/HCFO fluids such as R1336mzz(Z) — for high-temperature industrial heat-pump cycles in Aspen Plus V12.5. Compared coefficient of performance and volumetric heat capacity across temperature and pressure conditions against environmental and safety metrics (GWP, ODP), identifying R1336mzz(Z) and R245fa as the most efficient options for waste-heat recovery and decarbonization.

📈

PROCESS OPTIMIZATION · ASPEN PLUS SQP

Depropanizer + Debutanizer — Revenue Optimization

Formulated a two-column depropanizer–debutanizer NGL train as a constrained profit-maximization problem using the Aspen Plus SQP optimizer. Manipulated reflux and distillate-to-feed ratios under propane/butane purity and contamination constraints across five specification cases, showing that tightening overhead purity shifts product mass toward the higher-value C4 and C5+ streams and lifts revenue by roughly $0.38 M/yr over the unoptimized base case.

⚠️

PROCESS SAFETY · DNV PHAST v9.1

Chlorine & Propane Release — Consequence Analysis

Modeled four failure scenarios for pressurized chlorine and propane storage spheres in DNV PHAST v9.1 across three atmospheric stability classes. Quantified toxic dispersion, pool vaporization, thermal radiation, and vapor-cloud explosion hazards, showing that stability class can enlarge hazard zones by 10–16× and that sustained pool vaporization governs far-field consequences.

🧬

BIOPROCESS ENGINEERING · CHEN 5374

Downstream Processing of Therapeutic Antibodies

Critically analyzed modern downstream-processing strategies for monoclonal-antibody manufacturing — single-use systems, continuous chromatography, mechanistic modeling, and process analytical technology (PAT). Evaluated the yield, purity, cost-of-goods, and validation trade-offs of each, and proposed a pilot-scale benchmarking and FMEA-based validation strategy for choosing between continuous and batch purification.

📊

DATA MODELING · MATLAB

DEA Solvent Loss vs. Absorber Temperature

Fit linear, quadratic, and exponential regression models in MATLAB to relate diethanolamine (DEA) solvent loss to absorber temperature in a natural-gas sweetening unit. Used R² and residual analysis to select a quadratic model (R² ≈ 0.997) that balances predictive accuracy with physical interpretability, giving a basis for minimizing make-up-solvent cost.

🌡️

TRANSPORT PHENOMENA · CHEN 6302

Transient Heat Conduction in a Pipeline Wall

Solved the radial transient heat-conduction PDE for the wall of a buried steel crude-oil pipeline using a Crank–Nicolson finite-difference scheme in MATLAB. Modeled the time-dependent temperature profile through the pipe wall after a step change in crude temperature, capturing the approach to steady state under an insulated outer-wall boundary condition.

🤖

MACHINE LEARNING · MATLAB

Neural-Network Modeling of an Acid-Gas Absorber

Trained a feedforward neural network (MATLAB fitnet) on roughly 4,100 operating samples to predict sweet-gas flow rate and residual H₂S and CO₂ concentrations from six absorber inputs. Achieved under 1.6% error on held-out data and identified DEA concentration and feed temperature as the most influential variables, demonstrating a fast ANN surrogate for acid-gas absorber optimization.

⚗️

REACTION KINETICS · THERMODYNAMICS

CO₂ Transport-Pipeline Impurity Reactions

Analyzed the thermodynamics and kinetics of impurity reactions (H₂S, SO₂, NOₓ, NH₃, and water) in CO₂ transport pipelines for carbon-capture networks. Assessed redox, hydrolysis, and Claus reaction pathways for spontaneity under pipeline conditions and their role in forming corrosive species that threaten pipeline integrity.
